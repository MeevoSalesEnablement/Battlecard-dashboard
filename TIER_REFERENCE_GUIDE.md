## 🎯 Tier-Based Discovery Approach

### When Prospect Uses Enterprise Platform
*Lead with ease of use, support, and faster onboarding*
- "Most of our Enterprise customers switched because implementation took too long"
- "How responsive is your current support — phone or tickets?"
- "What's the learning curve for your staff?"

### When Prospect Uses Mid-Market Platform
*Lead with growth tools, reporting, and multi-location*
- "As you add locations, management gets complicated in most systems"
- "Can you pull real-time reporting, or do you wait for batch updates?"
- "What happens when fees start creeping as you add services?"

### When Prospect Uses SMB/Affordable Platform
*Lead with enterprise features and support quality*
- "You've got the affordability part down — now what about reliability?"
- "As you grow, can the system keep up?"
- "Are there hidden fees that show up later?"

### When Prospect Uses Med Spa Specialist
*Lead with breadth and operational completeness*
- "We handle salon + spa + medspa in one system — they only do one"
- "Beyond charting, can they manage retail, memberships, and payroll?"
- "How deep is their reporting for non-clinical side of the business?"

## 💡 Rep Tips by Tier

### Enterprise Prospects
- Lead with "easier, faster onboarding"
- Probe on support response times
- Ask about total cost of ownership (all modules + implementation)
- Emphasize simplicity for staff adoption

### Mid-Market Prospects
- Lead with "you can manage growth without complexity"
- Ask about real-time reporting capabilities
- Probe on hidden fees and add-on costs
- Emphasize multi-location capabilities

### SMB Prospects
- Lead with "reliability you can count on"
- Ask if system has crashed or caused double-charges
- Probe on what's actually included vs. paid add-ons
- Emphasize support quality vs. DIY solutions

### Med Spa Prospects
- Lead with "one system for your whole business"
- Ask depth of charting/EMR capabilities
- Probe on retail, membership, and payroll handling
- Emphasize compliance (SOC2, HIPAA, PCI)
